Screenz is a native macOS menu bar app that arranges your displays from a phone photo.

- Save named arrangements and restore them directly from the menu bar dropdown.
- Scan my desk is back in the dropdown, alongside rename/delete controls for saved setups.
- Saved setups match persistent display identities and use the same Keep/Revert confirmation.
- Pair over a QR code on the same local network; no phone app or account.
- Detect display markers locally with Apple Vision.
- Preview and adjust before applying, with a 20-second automatic revert.
- Optional Launch at Login.
- Universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel, requiring macOS 13 or later.

**These automated development builds are ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized.** macOS may block the first launch until you explicitly allow a trusted build in Privacy & Security. Maintainers should replace these assets with notarized packages before advertising a notarized release.

Download the DMG, drag Screenz to Applications, then open it and look for the two-display menu bar icon. ZIP and SHA-256 checksums are also provided.
