Screenz — Arrange your displays with a photo

1. Drag Screenz.app to Applications.
2. Open Screenz from Applications.
3. Look for the two-display icon in the menu bar.
4. Click the menu bar icon, choose Scan my desk, and scan the QR code.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
Your phone and Mac must be on the same local network.

Release notes indicate whether a build is notarized. For a non-notarized
development build you trust, macOS may require allowing it in System
Settings > Privacy & Security after the first attempt to open it.

Use Save current arrangement to name and save a setup. Saved titles
appear in the menu bar dropdown; select one to restore it.

Closing the window keeps Screenz in the menu bar. Click its icon for
saved arrangements, scanning, Quit Screenz, and Launch at Login.

Source, instructions, and issue reports:
